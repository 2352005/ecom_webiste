OUR ORIGIN

VORTEX was born in the neon-lit alleys of the digital age. We started as a collective of artists, hackers, and urban explorers who felt the current fashion landscape was static. We wanted to create pieces that move with the pulse of the generation.

THE EVOLUTION

From a bedroom studio to a global movement.

2021

The first "Zero-G" hoodie drop. Sold out in 12 seconds.

2022

Expanded to Tokyo and London. Launched the first digital-wear collection.

2023

VORTEX STUDIO opened. A creative hub for community-led design.
